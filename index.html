    Direct representation from Ratnapura, Elahera, and Nivithigala pits. Discussing ethical licensing, direct cooperative pricing, and eliminating predatory intermediary cut-outs.

  2. 02 / LAPIDARY ARTISANS

    CUT & POLISH FACETERS

    Sri Lanka’s premier lapidary artisans showcasing brilliant-cut, cushion, and bespoke fantasy cuts. Standardizing precision cutting tolerances for global luxury brands.

  3. 03 / BOURSE DEALERS

    AGENTS & CERTIFIED BROKERS

    The vital liquidity providers of Beruwala, Ratnapura, and Colombo bourses. Establishing transparent digital escrow, fair commission benchmarks, and verified certificates.

  4. 04 / GLOBAL EXPORTERS

    INTERNATIONAL TRADERS

    Exporters serving Tokyo, Hong Kong, Bangkok, Geneva, and New York. Addressing export taxation, direct air-freight insured logistics, and foreign currency trade facilities.

    Showcasing the latest Raman spectrometers, UV-Vis-NIR instruments, immersion cells, and high-precision diamond/gem balances to detect synthetic simulants.

    High-level policy dialogue between the Gemological Institute of America (GIA), German Gemmological Association (DGemG), Sri Lanka Gem & Jewellery Association (SLGJA), and NGJA.

Where does the networking gala take place?

The Grand Ballroom at Waters Edge, Battaramulla, Sri Lanka. Overlooking the serene Diyawanna Lake at twilight, offering world-class dining, security, and ballroom acoustics.

What is the five-hour gala choreography?

18:00 Arrival & Red Carpet Waterfront Reception; 18:45 Keynotes from international speakers and GIA / DGemG delegates; 19:30 Live Unveiling of the CAGSNET Digital Ecosystem; 20:15 Curated Multi-Course Dinner at designated supply chain tables; 21:15 Commemorative Souvenir Presentation and Charity Gemstone Draw Prize.

How are the supply chain dinner tables assigned?

Delegates are strictly categorized and seated according to their supply chain role (Miners, Cut & Polish Masters, Brokers, Exporters, Jewelers, Tech Vendors, and Institutional VIPs) to eliminate fragmentation and facilitate direct business transactions.

What is the official dress code?

Black-Tie Formal / Formal National Elegance. Attendees are requested to dress to the standard of a sovereign state banquet.
